re/read

Settings

Everything re/read does happens on this device (offline): translating, the dictionary, the reading list, your saved phrases. Nothing you read or select is ever sent anywhere. An internet connection is needed only when you want to download a new translation model or a dictionary - and only from the two URLs shown openly in those sections.

First steps

Nothing translates yet - the model that would do it is not yet on this device. Setting up takes a minute:

  1. Under "Translation models", find the language pair you need (the filter finds it fastest) and press "Download". The model is stored on this device and translates offline from then on - and the first model sets the language pair above by itself.
  2. Under "Dictionaries", download a dictionary for the same pair (optional, but worth it) - single words then get fuller answers than a translation alone (after pressing "More" in the translation bubble).

Last, pin re/read to the toolbar: the puzzle-piece icon at the right end of the bar, then the gear next to re/read and "Pin to Toolbar". The button opens the reader, the reading list, your saved phrases and the switch for the site you are on.

For reading in your own language. With this on, the translation bubble, saving and underlining phrases, the saved phrases page and the model section are hidden; selecting text on an ordinary page offers one thing - open the page in the reader. The reader, the offline reading list, the highlighter, reading aloud, search and dictionaries keep working. Nothing is deleted: your phrases and models come back the moment you switch this off.

The pairs offered are those whose translation models you have downloaded to this device - if you want to translate in another language pair, download its model under Translation models.

The voice reads a selected phrase in the bubble and a whole article in the reader. The voices are this device's own: speech is made locally and nothing is sent anywhere.

Translation models

The models below are built and published by Mozilla - the same models Firefox itself translates pages with. For re/read to work, download a model for the language pairs you want to translate (for example en -> pl).

List from -

Model downloads come from - - where Mozilla publishes them. Updating the list sends nothing about you; it only fetches the fresh list. This host and the dictionary catalogue's below are the only two this extension ever contacts, and only when you press to refresh a list or to download something.

Add a model from files

To add by hand a model the list above does not offer, load its files here.

Pick the three files of one direction and they are stored locally. Gzipped files are unpacked here, so the downloads can be used as they come:

  • model.enpl.intgemm.alphas.bin
  • lex.50.50.enpl.s2t.bin
  • vocab.enpl.spm

Adding a model by hand makes no network request, and this page stays the way in on the day the place models are downloaded from stops answering.

Dictionaries

Download a dictionary (or a few) to check the different meanings a selected word can have. While translating you choose and save the senses that fit the context; with translation switched off, the definitions simply answer a selection in the reader.

List from -

The bubble answers in the order below - the arrows move a dictionary up or down. Only the dictionaries of the language you are reading answer at all.

Dictionary downloads come from - - where WikDict publishes the dictionaries it builds from Wiktionary (CC BY-SA). When you update the list, nothing about you is sent - the request only fetches the fresh list.

Add a dictionary from files

To add a dictionary from your own files - one from an e-reader works as it is - load them here.

Dictionaries are read in the StarDict format - the same files a KOReader e-reader uses, so a dictionary already on your reader works here unchanged. Unpack the archive it came in and pick the files of one dictionary:

  • name.ifo - what the dictionary calls itself
  • name.idx - the index of words
  • name.dict or name.dict.dz - the entries
  • name.syn - other spellings, when the dictionary has them

A monolingual dictionary works too - for English-English, say, pick English on both sides. Dictionaries are matched by the language of their headwords, not by the pair, so it answers beside the bilingual ones: under "More" while translating, straight away with translation off.

A StarDict file does not say which languages it is for, so that part is yours to fill in. Adding from files makes no network request; files from anywhere work, the catalogue's own included - they are published at download.wikdict.com/dictionaries/stardict.

Switched-off sites

Sites where re/read stays off: no underlines, no bubble, nothing running on the page itself; opening a page in the reader by hand still works. The switch in the toolbar popup is what adds a site here; this list is how one comes back on.
