Translation models

A model is what turns one language into another, and there is one per direction. Nothing is translated until the model for the pair you are reading is here, on this device. Every direction Mozilla publishes is listed; the pair you are reading and anything already stored float to the top.

Downloading a model is the only time this extension uses the network, and it is the only request it will ever make. The files come from -, where Mozilla publishes them, at addresses written down in this package. Every file is checked against a checksum that was recorded here on -: anything that does not match is thrown away rather than stored. Nothing about you is sent - the request says which model, and nothing else.

Add a model from files

Pick the three files of one direction and they are stored locally. Gzipped files are unpacked here, so the downloads can be used as they come:

  • model.enpl.intgemm.alphas.bin
  • lex.50.50.enpl.s2t.bin
  • vocab.enpl.spm

This page stays even once models download themselves: it is the way out on the day the place they are downloaded from stops answering. Nothing here leaves the device, and adding a model makes no network request at all.

Dictionaries

A dictionary answers what a translation cannot. Bank has two entries, and a model has to choose one of them without knowing which you meant; a dictionary shows both. Entries appear in the second layer of the bubble, under the sentence.

Add a dictionary from files

Dictionaries are read in StarDict format - the same files a KOReader e-reader uses, so a dictionary already on your reader works here unchanged. Unpack the archive it came in and pick the files of one dictionary:

  • name.ifo - what the dictionary calls itself
  • name.idx - the index of words
  • name.dict or name.dict.dz - the entries
  • name.syn - other spellings, when the dictionary has them

A StarDict file says nothing about which languages it is for, so that part is yours to say. Dictionaries are never downloaded by this extension: there is no list of addresses for them anywhere in this package, and adding one makes no network request at all. The ones worth having are WikDict's (from Wiktionary, CC BY-SA) - StarDict files for dozens of pairs, published at download.wikdict.com/dictionaries/stardict. Download one, unpack it, and pick its files above.

Switched-off sites

Sites where re/read stays off - no underlines, no bubble, nothing running. The switch in the toolbar popup is what adds a site here; this list is how one is turned back on without visiting it.
