re/read

Settings

Everything re/read does happens on this device: translating, the dictionary, the reading list, your saved phrases. Nothing you read or select is ever sent anywhere. The network serves two things, both on this page and both at your press: downloading a model or a dictionary, and updating the list of models. Always from the two addresses written into this package, and nothing else.

First steps

Nothing translates yet - the model that would do it is not on this device. Setting up takes a minute:

  1. Set the language pair just below: the language you read, and the language it is translated into.
  2. Under "Translation models", press "Download" next to that pair. The model is stored on this device and translates offline from then on.
  3. Worth the second press: a dictionary for the same pair, under "Get a dictionary" - single words then get fuller answers than a translation alone.

Last, pin re/read to the toolbar - until you do, its button sits behind the puzzle-piece icon. Behind the button: the reader, the offline reading list, your saved phrases and a switch for the site you are on.

Saved phrases

Translation models

The models below are built and published by Mozilla - the same models Firefox itself translates pages with. For re/read to work, download a model for the language pairs you want to translate (for example en -> pl).

- the list of translation models below is from -. Press the button to fetch the current list.

Model downloads come from - - where Mozilla publishes them. When you update the list, nothing about you is sent - the request only fetches the fresh list of models. This host and the dictionary catalogue's below are the only two places this extension can ever contact - and only when you knowingly press to refresh the list, to download a model or a dictionary.

Add a model from files

To add by hand a model the list above does not offer, load its files here.

Pick the three files of one direction and they are stored locally. Gzipped files are unpacked here, so the downloads can be used as they come:

  • model.enpl.intgemm.alphas.bin
  • lex.50.50.enpl.s2t.bin
  • vocab.enpl.spm

This page stays even once models download themselves: it is the way out on the day the place they are downloaded from stops answering. Nothing here leaves the device, and adding a model makes no network request at all.

Dictionaries

A dictionary answers what a translation cannot. Bank has two entries, and a model has to choose one of them without knowing which you meant; a dictionary shows both. Entries appear in the second layer of the bubble, under the sentence.

Get a dictionary

WikDict builds dictionaries from Wiktionary (CC BY-SA) for every pair below. One press downloads the archive, unpacks it here and stores it; the languages are read off the pair, and the dictionary appears in the list above, ready to answer.

Dictionary downloads come from - - where WikDict publishes them - at addresses written down in this package; the list was checked here on - - unlike models there is no checksum to hold them to: WikDict rebuilds its files in place, so a sum recorded here would break with every rebuild. What stands in its place: the download must be a plain zip of StarDict files, it is read as warily as any file you would add by hand, and what it becomes is text in the local database - never code. Nothing about you is sent: the request says which dictionary, and nothing else.

Add a dictionary from files

To add a dictionary from your own files - one from an e-reader works as it is - load them here.

Dictionaries are read in the StarDict format - the same files a KOReader e-reader uses, so a dictionary already on your reader works here unchanged. Unpack the archive it came in and pick the files of one dictionary:

  • name.ifo - what the dictionary calls itself
  • name.idx - the index of words
  • name.dict or name.dict.dz - the entries
  • name.syn - other spellings, when the dictionary has them

A StarDict file says nothing about which languages it is for, so here that part is yours to say. Adding from files makes no network request at all, and the path stays for the day the catalogue's host stops answering: files from anywhere work, the catalogue's own included - they are published at download.wikdict.com/dictionaries/stardict.

Switched-off sites

Sites where re/read stays off - no underlines, no bubble, nothing running. The switch in the toolbar popup is what adds a site here; this list is how one is turned back on without visiting it.
