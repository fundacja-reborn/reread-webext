re/read

Settings

Everything re/read does happens on this device (offline): translating, the dictionary, the reading list, your saved phrases. Nothing you read or select is ever sent anywhere. An internet connection is needed only when you want to download a new translation model or a dictionary - and only from the two URLs shown openly in those sections.

First steps

Nothing translates yet - the model that would do it is not yet on this device. Setting up takes a minute:

  1. Under "Translation models", find the language pair you need (the filter finds it fastest) and press "Download". The model is stored on this device and translates offline from then on - and the first model sets the language pair above by itself.
  2. Under "Dictionaries", download a dictionary for the same pair (optional, but worth it) - single words then get fuller answers than a translation alone (after pressing "More" in the translation bubble).

Last, pin re/read to the toolbar: press the puzzle-piece icon at the right end of the bar and, next to re/read, choose the gear and "Pin to Toolbar". The re/read button gives quick access to the reader, the offline reading list, your saved phrases, changing the language pair and the switch for the site you are on.

The pairs offered are those whose translation models you have downloaded to this device - if you want to translate in another language pair, download its model under Translation models.

Engine Bergamot, inside the package - translates offline

The speaker button in the translation bubble reads the selected phrase aloud in the language you are reading - the way to hear how a word is pronounced. The voices are this device's and its browser's own: speech is made locally, and the phrase is not sent anywhere. With no choice made, the browser picks a voice for the language itself.

With this on, selecting a phrase or a word on an ordinary page offers one thing: open the page in the reader. Translating, saving and underlining happen there (cleaner and easier to read). On Android the option is on by default.

With this on, the translation bubble shows just the translation, and its buttons slide out when you click or tap the bubble. Save is the exception either way: a phrase too long to save itself always shows the button that saves it.

Translation models

The models below are built and published by Mozilla - the same models Firefox itself translates pages with. For re/read to work, download a model for the language pairs you want to translate (for example en -> pl).

List from -

Model downloads come from - - where Mozilla publishes them. When you update the list, nothing about you is sent - the request only fetches the fresh list of models. This host and the dictionary catalogue's below are the only two places this extension can ever contact - and only when you knowingly press to refresh a list, to download a model or a dictionary.

Add a model from files

To add by hand a model the list above does not offer, load its files here.

Pick the three files of one direction and they are stored locally. Gzipped files are unpacked here, so the downloads can be used as they come:

  • model.enpl.intgemm.alphas.bin
  • lex.50.50.enpl.s2t.bin
  • vocab.enpl.spm

This page stays even once models download themselves: it is the way out on the day the place they are downloaded from stops answering. Nothing here leaves the device, and adding a model makes no network request at all.

Dictionaries

Download a dictionary (or a few) to check the different meanings a selected word can have - you then choose and save the senses that fit the context.

List from -

Dictionary downloads come from - - where WikDict publishes the dictionaries it builds from Wiktionary (CC BY-SA). When you update the list, nothing about you is sent - the request only fetches the fresh list.

Add a dictionary from files

To add a dictionary from your own files - one from an e-reader works as it is - load them here.

Dictionaries are read in the StarDict format - the same files a KOReader e-reader uses, so a dictionary already on your reader works here unchanged. Unpack the archive it came in and pick the files of one dictionary:

  • name.ifo - what the dictionary calls itself
  • name.idx - the index of words
  • name.dict or name.dict.dz - the entries
  • name.syn - other spellings, when the dictionary has them

A StarDict file says nothing about which languages it is for, so here that part is yours to say. Adding from files makes no network request at all, and the path stays for the day the catalogue's host stops answering: files from anywhere work, the catalogue's own included - they are published at download.wikdict.com/dictionaries/stardict.

Switched-off sites

Sites where re/read stays off - no underlines, no bubble, nothing running on the page itself; opening a page in the reader by hand still works. The switch in the toolbar popup is what adds a site here; this list is how one is turned back on without visiting it.
