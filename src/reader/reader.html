How it works

Everything here is a copy stored on this device - it opens without a connection and stays even if the original page goes away.

    File format

    One .json file holding every saved article - a backup, or the way to move them to another browser. Importing adds articles this list is missing and never touches a saved one.

    Books stay out of this file: the backup of a book is its .epub - importing it again brings everything back except the reading position.
