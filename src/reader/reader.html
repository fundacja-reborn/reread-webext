Reader

The reader is not built yet. It will take the article off the page you are on, run it through Readability, and render it here - a clean document where highlighting is exact rather than best effort, with typography and themes under this extension's control and no network involved.

Firefox's own reader lives on an about: page, which extensions cannot touch. That is why this page exists rather than a button that opens that one.
